# Code of conduct

Hokusai follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment: being empathetic and kind, respecting differing
opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes
and learning from them, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others'
private information without explicit permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/ide-bloom/hokusai/security/advisories/new) marked "Code of conduct",
or by contacting a maintainer listed on the repository. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy is respected. Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors
whose behavior violates this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.

## Scope

This code applies in all community spaces and when an individual officially represents the community in public
spaces.
